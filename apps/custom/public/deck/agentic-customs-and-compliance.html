Agentic operations

Command Center & Qubere AI Copilot

The operational control plane combines shipment readiness, agent decisions, exceptions, tasks, regulatory signals, and a grounded compliance copilot.

  • Shipment readiness from real line items, values, classifications and required/missing documents
  • Agent decisions summarized into verified, needs-review and blocked states
  • Severity-driven exception queues with assignments, reviewer actions and formal overrides
  • Regulatory signals and open compliance deadlines surfaced into daily operations
Operating principle
The Copilot is grounded in persisted Qubere evidence; it does not calculate or replace deterministic regulatory decisions.

Customs execution

Customs Filing & Document Intelligence

Qubere connects document intake, shipment data, classification, compliance checks, exceptions and filing readiness in one Agentic Customs workflow.

  • AI-assisted extraction and normalization of commercial and logistics documents
  • Missing-data, document and exception checks determine filing readiness from actual shipment state
  • Country-specific filing payloads and authority submission/response via jurisdiction connectors
  • Blocked or ambiguous conditions route to reviewers rather than being silently auto-cleared

Decision intelligence

Product Classification & Product Master

Classification is a dedicated customs decision capability grounded in tariff reference data and controlled by confidence and review policy.

  • GRI-oriented HTS classification using product descriptions, tariff data, confidence and evidence
  • Reusable product identity, descriptions, classifications and compliance attributes
  • Duty and tariff evidence comes from ingested reference data, never model invention
  • Classification decisions remain independently persisted, explainable and reportable

Core compliance

Restricted Party Screening & Pre-Approved Parties

One canonical RPS capability supports Party Master, shipment, API, Community Screening, Bulk Screening and Continuous Party Monitoring.

  • Fuzzy name/address screening with thresholds, country evidence and source/list provenance
  • Red-flag indicators remain visible independently of a restricted-party match
  • Matches can be assigned, reviewed, resolved and formally overridden without losing the original result
  • Fail-closed pre-approval reuse based on account, party version, and reference-data freshness
Operating principle
Technical failures, stale approvals or insufficient evidence never become CLEAR.

Deterministic controls

Embargo, Forced-Labor & End-Use Controls

Qubere combines deterministic country controls with specialized compliance checks and exposes rule source, severity, evidence and remediation context.

  • Compliance/ship-from country evaluated against destination/origin using active rule data
  • Account-scoped private embargo rules augment public/system controls with clear provenance
  • UFLPA/forced-labor reference data surfaces dedicated findings and evidence
  • Explicit gap states prevent unavailable data or rules from masquerading as clearance

Authorization controls

License Determination & License Management

Regulatory determination, managed-license applicability and utilization remain separate so every step is explainable and auditable.

  • Uses classification, jurisdiction, end-use/end-user, encryption and applicable control data
  • Exception codes apply only when all required regulatory conditions are satisfied
  • Original regulatory outcome preserved when exceptions or overrides affect the final decision
  • Full utilization ledger — licensed, committed, shipped, adjusted and remaining — with idempotent events
Operating principle
Incomplete rule data, invalid classification or system error cannot become NO_LICENSE_REQUIRED.

Proactive monitoring

Continuous Party Monitoring (RDPS)

Reference-data changes identify potentially affected Party Master records, which are re-screened through canonical RPS.

  • Published additions, updates, removals, supersessions and expirations become auditable change sets
  • Names, aliases, addresses, countries and lifecycle changes identify candidate parties
  • Candidate retrieval can over-select for safety; final decisions always come from canonical RPS
  • Material worsening transitions can create alerts, tasks, email and review work

Trusted content

Reference Data & Regulatory Intelligence

Trusted, versioned reference content underpins deterministic compliance and grounded Agentic Customs experiences.

  • BIS screening-list ingestion and DHS UFLPA forced-labor entity reference data

Controlled intervention

Governance, Audit, Notifications & Usage

Qubere separates automated decisions, human actions and operational side effects while preserving a defensible evidence chain.

  • Execution records link request context, domain results, findings, versions and correlation IDs
  • Append-only audit history captures lifecycle actions, reviews, approvals and revocations
  • Original decision, override decision, reason, actor and timestamp remain visible
  • Provider-neutral queue/dispatch architecture for screening, pre-approval and license notifications
Operating principle
Notification, reporting and usage-metering failures never change the authoritative compliance decision.

Evidence and security

Compliance Reporting & Enterprise Controls

Qubere turns persisted compliance evidence into operational and audit-ready reporting while enforcing tenant authorization independently of report filters.

  • Compliance audit reports: execution, findings, reviewer actions and override evidence
  • Screening & monitoring reports: RPS, Embargo, Party Compliance, RDPS and Reference Data Changes
  • Classification & license reports, plus saved/scheduled report definitions with run history
  • Account-scoped data access and permission-gated sensitive actions across APIs and UI
Operating principle
Generated files are report artifacts, not authoritative records; reports query persisted domain evidence and do not recalculate compliance outcomes.
