What's real, and where the data stops

Say it plainly.

✓ Simulator: standard duty/MPF + Section 301 — strongest for origin-shift and 301 questions
✓ Recovery models built, real Decimal math, over-allocation guards
✓ Regulatory feed: Federal Register (CBP notices), live
○ Section 301 exclusion-text matching needs the exclusion dataset
○ Precise PSC deadlines need CBP liquidation dates (ACE connection)
○ Automated AD/CVD rate/scope — not yet

Unknowns show as "unknown," never a computed guess. That honesty is the trust argument.

Objection handling

Every hard question, answered directly.

"Do you file the refund with CBP?"Qubere identifies opportunities, matches drawback lots, and produces the preparation package (claim / PSC / protest). The ACE/CBP submission goes through the filing path (live with ABI credentials) or the broker's system. It's the analysis and the package, done systematically — not an automatic wire.
"How real is the recovery detection?"Models and workflows are built with real Decimal math and over-allocation guards. Two honest data gaps: Section 301 exclusion-text matching and precise PSC deadlines — both show "unknown" rather than a guess.
"Is the regulatory feed comprehensive?"Federal Register CBP notices, live. USITC trade-remedy orders, §232/§301 rate annexes, PGA requirements are on the data roadmap — and the platform is explicit about which datasets are LIVE vs not.
