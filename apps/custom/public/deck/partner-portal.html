How Qubere addresses it

A read-and-respond surface for your clients — scoped, not filtered.

  • The customer sees only their shipments, documents, entries, invoices — enforced on every read path, not hidden in the UI.
  • "Ask the customer" is a request raised against a specific shipment, tracked end to end.
  • A document the customer uploads enters the same parse → extract → reconcile → readiness pipeline as a broker upload.
  • Entry visibility is an explicit publish action by the broker — nothing leaks early.
  • Passwordless, scoped invitations — access limited to one client and optionally specific product scopes.

"Ask the customer" request workflow

Chasing a document is now a tracked request, not an email thread.

The broker raises a document/data request against a specific shipment from /app/clients; the customer gets an email, opens it in the portal, and answers or uploads. Status tracked the whole way.

Passwordless, scoped invitations

The customer sees exactly what you decide, when you decide.

The broker sends a scoped invitation (/invite/<token>); the customer accepts and is provisioned with access limited to their client and, optionally, specific product scopes. For enterprises: the same mechanism scoped to a supplier and their POs.

  • Invitation states: PENDING / ACCEPTED / EXPIRED / REVOKED
  • Portal runs as its own origin

The security model, plainly

What the customer cannot do.

  • See other clients' data — client scope is enforced server-side on every read path.
  • See unpublished entries — visibility is an explicit broker action.
  • Take compliance actions or reach the broker's internal workspace.
  • Access anything through a guessed URL — the portal is a separate origin with its own auth.

The security model was hardened through a formal review. It's a read + respond surface, nothing more.

Objection handling

Every hard question, answered directly.

"Is this production-ready?"Pilot-ready. Auth, client scoping, and object storage are real and were hardened through a formal review. The remaining gap is polished generated-PDF downloads and broader automated two-tenant test coverage — fine for a design partner, worth being explicit about for a large rollout.
"Can we brand it per broker?"The portal uses the Qubere design system today. Custom per-broker branding is a roadmap item, not current.
"How do suppliers fit the enterprise story?"Same mechanism — an invitation scoped to a party and product scopes — used for supplier document and origin-data collection rather than importer status visibility.
