The problem

Billing is retrospective, and revenue leaks silently.

What a brokerage owner says:

"The rate card lives in a spreadsheet. Maybe three. Nobody's sure which version was in effect for an entry that closed six weeks ago."

Ungoverned rates

"A classification override, a supervisor's second review, an exception someone resolved by phone — none of it shows up as billable time or internal cost."

Invisible work

"Restricted-party screening is real labor and real risk transfer. It's a checkbox in our workflow, never a line on the invoice."

Free compliance

How Qubere addresses it

Billing that doesn't wait for someone to remember to do it.

  • Every operational event emits a usage record automatically — there is no manual keying step in this flow.
  • Rate cards are versioned, immutable once activated, with a draft → active → retired lifecycle.
  • Duty, MPF, HMF, taxes are pulled from actual filing totals — not estimated, not zeroed — and separated from broker revenue.
  • Human review carries a real measured duration. Every compliance screen emits its own rated event.
  • Exception detection flags unmapped rates, negative margins, and unbilled work — and it's actionable.

Objection handling

Every hard question, answered directly.

"Does it replace QuickBooks / Xero?"No — it's the operational billing layer that feeds a clean, itemized, defensible invoice into whatever you use for AR and books. The value is the invoice is right and traceable before it leaves Qubere.
"Credit notes / charge disputes?"A formal credit-note / dispute workflow is not built yet — the DISPUTED / CREDITED states exist but aren't wired end to end. Adjustments-with-reason on a charge are the current mechanism. This is the one real gap.
"How do we know the usage numbers are right?"Cross-tenant billing isolation has automated test coverage, and every rated event links to the operational record that generated it — you can always click through to the proof.
