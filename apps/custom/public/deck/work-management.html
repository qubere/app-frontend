The problem

Work lives in a dozen inboxes, and you find out it's stuck when the client calls.

What an operations manager at a brokerage says:

"Every morning someone spends an hour deciding who does what. We missed an ISF deadline last quarter because an entry was sitting in someone's queue."

Triage & deadlines

"The AI tools we've looked at are a smarter classifier. They don't actually move the file — a person still babysits every step."

Not autonomous

"Two people worked the same entry last week. Neither knew the other was on it. I can't tell you which of my open entries are past due right now."

No visibility

How Qubere addresses it

The pipeline, the gates, the SLAs, the escalations — that's the product.

  • The AI does the analysis inside each stage; the workflow engine moves the entry between them.
  • One queue, ranked automatically by filing deadline, dollars at risk, and whether the item blocks other work.
  • The brokerage sets exactly where a licensed person must sign off — and that gate cannot be cleared by anyone else.
  • You dial in how much the AI may auto-approve. Above your line is auto-verified and fully audited; below waits for a person.
  • An untouched high-value item surfaces to the manager automatically after a set number of hours.

Routed work queue

One prioritized list. The sort is the triage.

Ranked automatically by filing deadline, declared value at risk, and downstream blocking. My / Team / Unassigned / All views — reviewers see only their work; a lead sees what nobody owns and hands it out. No two people double-handling one entry.

  • Client auto-routing: a client's shipments land with the broker who owns the account
  • Multi-select → Assign → teammate; one notification

Human gates & the auto-approval policy engine

You decide where a licensed person signs off — and it's enforced.

Set Compliance = "Human gate, Licensed Broker." A non-broker cannot clear it, and the approval is on the record. Separately, drag an agent's confidence threshold from 85% → 95% and the same decision now needs a human confirm.

  • Stage gates configurable per stage and per entry type
  • Agent policies: confidence threshold + "part-master match must agree"
  • All self-service in Settings — no deployment

SLA clocks & escalation

Nothing important sits unowned past a deadline.

Every review gets a due time from its priority: on time / due soon / breached +Nh. An untouched, unassigned high-value item surfaces to the team manager automatically after a set number of hours — email + in-app — and the event is logged.

  • Escalation rules: "SLA breach + 2h → team manager"
  • Exception workbench: structured resolution reasons, one at a time or bulk

Objection handling

Every hard question, answered directly.

"Does it file, or just tell my staff what to do?"It advances the shipment autonomously through analysis stages and produces the validated entry. The human gates are where you decide a licensed person signs off. Live CBP transmission activates with ABI filer credentials.
"What if the AI auto-approves something wrong?"Every auto-verification is logged with the policy version and confidence, and is reversible. You set the threshold — start at 95%+ and loosen it as you trust it. Nothing auto-approves below your line.
"We already have a TMS / workflow tool."Qubere's queue is driven by customs readiness and filing deadlines specifically — not a generic task tracker. It complements a TMS; the same engine runs the freight side if you want both.
