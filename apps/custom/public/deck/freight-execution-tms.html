How Qubere addresses it

Additive intelligence. The dispatcher works exceptions.

  • Every agent operates under an Additive Intelligence Mandate — raw metadata, contacts, dates, move types, unmapped fields all captured; no step discards prior facts.
  • Extracted facts are promoted onto the operational shipment and transportation-order rows — route, tracking refs, equipment, cargo lines.
  • Document and movement readiness are evaluated continuously; gaps become exceptions automatically.
  • A policy engine audits linehaul and drayage quotes against approved target margins.
  • Health status — Healthy / At Risk / Critical — from tracking freshness, promise buffers, LFD risk, open exceptions.

Agents 1–2 · Intake & enrichment

The document's facts become the shipment's facts — nothing dropped.

Intake classifies the document and extracts 100% of visible freight facts with provenance — including raw key-value pairs, cut-off/ETD/ETA dates, move types, vessel/voyage, line items, unmapped fields. Enrichment promotes route, MBL/HBL/booking/container, equipment, and cargo onto the operational rows.

  • Downstream agents build on accumulatedData, never re-parse
  • The opposite of "extract the 6 fields we mapped, drop the rest"

One platform, movement to entry

The customs broker doesn't get a shoebox of PDFs.

Because enrichment already promoted route, parties, container, and cargo lines with provenance, the customs side starts from structured, evidence-backed data. Multi-leg is the shared model. For a forwarder who also needs customs, the enrichment work is done once and the broker inherits it — same identity, same design system, same document and leg models.

Objection handling

Every hard question, answered directly.

"Do you connect to carrier EDI / ocean visibility / telematics?"The pipeline consumes tracking events and documents that arrive; the exception workbench references EDI 214 and telematics as event sources. Packaged direct integrations to specific carriers/providers are a scoped implementation item — confirm the carrier before committing.
"Is this a full TMS — rating, routing guide, appointments?"There's rating, tendering, movement/stop/appointment modeling, and a margin policy engine. It is not a mature load-board / brokerage-TMS replacement for a large 3PL with complex routing guides — position it as autonomous execution and exception management, strongest for forwarder/import flows.
"How does it relate to the customs app?"Same monorepo, same identity, same design system, shared document and (Phase 1) leg models. Sold and deployed together or standalone.
