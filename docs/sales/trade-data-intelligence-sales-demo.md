# Qubere Trade Data & Intelligence — Sales Product Guide

> Audience: Trade advisory · classification · procurement. This guide is based on the current repository and is intended for discovery, product explanation, and live demonstrations.

## Positioning

Reuse trusted product, party, tariff, and regulatory knowledge across every shipment and decision.

Use the customer pain first. Do not start by listing screens. Ask how the prospect handles the problem today, quantify the operational consequence, and then show the shortest product path that resolves it.

## Demo preparation

- Use an account with seeded shipments, documents, parties, and the permissions required for this category.
- Confirm the feature status shown below before a prospect call. “Roadmap” must never be presented as currently live.
- Keep one clean anchor record open before the call; avoid searching for a usable record in front of the prospect.
- After each feature, return to the customer outcome: time removed, risk reduced, revenue protected, or visibility gained.

## Product Master

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

The same product is reclassified and re-researched on every shipment because prior knowledge is trapped in entry files.

### Customer benefit

A tenant-scoped product master preserves identifiers, attributes, classifications, evidence, decisions, and change history.

### How to demo

1. Trade Data
2. Products
3. open a product
4. show identifiers, attributes, classification history, evidence, and shipment use.

**What to say:** “A tenant-scoped product master preserves identifiers, attributes, classifications, evidence, decisions, and change history.”

## Party Master

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Supplier, manufacturer, importer, consignee, and contact data is duplicated with inconsistent names and screening history.

### Customer benefit

A party master centralizes legal identity, addresses, roles, identifiers, relationships, screening, and approval history.

### How to demo

1. Trade Data
2. Parties
3. open a party
4. show identity, addresses, identifiers, screening history, and linked shipments.

**What to say:** “A party master centralizes legal identity, addresses, roles, identifiers, relationships, screening, and approval history.”

## Tariff & Sourcing Simulator

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Teams commit to sourcing decisions without understanding the full duty stack or alternative-country exposure.

### Customer benefit

Model base duty, special tariffs, fees, and sourcing scenarios before the purchase order is signed.

### How to demo

1. Trade Data
2. Tariff Simulator
3. enter product, origin, destination, and value
4. compare scenarios.

**What to say:** “Model base duty, special tariffs, fees, and sourcing scenarios before the purchase order is signed.”

## Regulatory Intelligence

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Policy updates arrive as long publications, leaving teams to determine which products and shipments are affected.

### Customer benefit

Qubere captures regulatory changes, summarizes operational meaning, and connects them to impacted trade data.

### How to demo

1. Trade Data
2. Tariffs & Regulations
3. open an update
4. show summary, effective date, affected codes, and impacted products.

**What to say:** “Qubere captures regulatory changes, summarizes operational meaning, and connects them to impacted trade data.”

## Trade Data Workspace

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Classification, party, tariff, and shipment insights are stored in separate systems and cannot be explored together.

### Customer benefit

A shared data workspace connects product and party masters, tariffs, import activity, and reusable reporting.

### How to demo

1. Trade Data
2. Overview
3. filter by product, party, country, or HTS
4. open the linked master record or shipment.

**What to say:** “A shared data workspace connects product and party masters, tariffs, import activity, and reusable reporting.”

## Sales guardrails

- Do not invent ROI, accuracy, throughput, or risk-reduction percentages. Use the prospect’s baseline and approved Qubere evidence.
- If seeded data or an external connector is absent, explain the intended flow and use the product deck rather than pretending the live action completed.
