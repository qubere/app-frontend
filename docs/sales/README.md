# Qubere Sales Enablement

This directory trains sellers on the product by category. Each guide uses the same sequence: customer pain, customer benefit, and an exact product path to demonstrate the capability.

## Category guides

- [Customs](./customs-sales-demo.md) — Move an entry from shipment intake to filing readiness in one connected workspace.
- [Compliance](./compliance-sales-demo.md) — Turn screening from a filing-time checkbox into continuous, defensible risk control.
- [Security](./security-sales-demo.md) — Protect tenant data and sensitive trade actions without slowing operators down.
- [Partner Portal](./partner-portal-sales-demo.md) — Collect clean inputs from clients and partners without turning every request into an email chase.
- [AI & Agents](./ai-agents-sales-demo.md) — Use AI to do the repetitive trade work while keeping evidence, confidence, and human control visible.
- [Document Management](./document-management-sales-demo.md) — Turn every inbound trade document into trusted, linked, searchable operational data.
- [Billing](./billing-sales-demo.md) — Convert operational work into explainable charges, protected margin, and controlled invoices.
- [Work Management](./work-management-sales-demo.md) — Give every operator a prioritized queue with a clear owner, deadline, context, and next action.
- [Multi-Leg Shipments](./multileg-shipments-sales-demo.md) — Keep master/house relationships, transport legs, tracking events, and customs context connected.
- [User Management](./user-management-sales-demo.md) — Onboard the right people into the right accounts and roles—then keep access understandable.
- [Platform](./platform-sales-demo.md) — Configure countries, workflows, integrations, reference data, and tenant operations on one extensible foundation.
- [Trade Data & Intelligence](./trade-data-intelligence-sales-demo.md) — Reuse trusted product, party, tariff, and regulatory knowledge across every shipment and decision.

## Browser decks

The in-product deck library is served from `apps/custom/public/deck/index.html`. Clicking the Qubere logo opens the library. Each category deck contains one slide per major feature and uses current product screenshots or product-faithful reconstructions.

## Seller rule

Never sell a roadmap state as live. The deck and guide explicitly label partial, architecture-dependent, and roadmap capabilities.
