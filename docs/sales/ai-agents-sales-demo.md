# Qubere AI & Agents — Sales Product Guide

> Audience: Operations · product · technology buyers. This guide is based on the current repository and is intended for discovery, product explanation, and live demonstrations.

## Positioning

Use AI to do the repetitive trade work while keeping evidence, confidence, and human control visible.

Use the customer pain first. Do not start by listing screens. Ask how the prospect handles the problem today, quantify the operational consequence, and then show the shortest product path that resolves it.

## Demo preparation

- Use an account with seeded shipments, documents, parties, and the permissions required for this category.
- Confirm the feature status shown below before a prospect call. “Roadmap” must never be presented as currently live.
- Keep one clean anchor record open before the call; avoid searching for a usable record in front of the prospect.
- After each feature, return to the customer outcome: time removed, risk reduced, revenue protected, or visibility gained.

## Ask Qubere

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Operational answers require jumping across shipments, documents, exceptions, tariffs, and audit records.

### Customer benefit

A permission-aware assistant retrieves grounded account data, cites the records it used, and navigates users to action.

### How to demo

1. Ask Qubere
2. ask what is blocking a shipment
3. expand sources
4. follow a suggested navigation action.

**What to say:** “A permission-aware assistant retrieves grounded account data, cites the records it used, and navigates users to action.”

## Document Intelligence

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Teams re-key the same invoice, packing-list, and transport data into multiple systems.

### Customer benefit

Qubere classifies, parses, chunks, extracts, and quality-checks trade documents before proposing field-level facts.

### How to demo

1. Docs
2. open processed document
3. show classification, extracted fields, confidence, evidence location, and review state.

**What to say:** “Qubere classifies, parses, chunks, extracts, and quality-checks trade documents before proposing field-level facts.”

## Universal Field Hydration

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Hand-maintained key mappings break whenever a new document field or destination schema appears.

### Customer benefit

A candidate-and-evidence engine maps extracted facts to canonical and filing fields, validates them, and records lineage.

### How to demo

1. Open shipment
2. Canonical Facts
3. select a hydrated field
4. show source extraction, mapping decision, confidence, and corrections.

**What to say:** “A candidate-and-evidence engine maps extracted facts to canonical and filing fields, validates them, and records lineage.”

## Agentic Shipment Pipeline

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Automation scripts run as isolated black boxes, leaving operators unsure what ran, what failed, and what is next.

### Customer benefit

An orchestrated sequence coordinates intake, document, product, classification, origin, compliance, readiness, and filing agents.

### How to demo

1. Open shipment
2. pipeline ribbon / agent timeline
3. expand an invocation
4. show inputs, outputs, status, duration, and next dependency.

**What to say:** “An orchestrated sequence coordinates intake, document, product, classification, origin, compliance, readiness, and filing agents.”

## Explainable Classification

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

A bare HTS code gives reviewers no confidence and creates weak reasonable-care evidence.

### Customer benefit

Classification cases combine candidates, GRI reasoning, supporting evidence, confidence, and explicit human decisions.

### How to demo

1. Trade Data
2. Products
3. open product
4. Classification Case
5. compare candidates
6. accept or override with reason.

**What to say:** “Classification cases combine candidates, GRI reasoning, supporting evidence, confidence, and explicit human decisions.”

## Sales guardrails

- Do not invent ROI, accuracy, throughput, or risk-reduction percentages. Use the prospect’s baseline and approved Qubere evidence.
- If seeded data or an external connector is absent, explain the intended flow and use the product deck rather than pretending the live action completed.
