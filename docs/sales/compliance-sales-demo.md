# Qubere Compliance — Sales Product Guide

> Audience: Compliance leaders · brokers · importers. This guide is based on the current repository and is intended for discovery, product explanation, and live demonstrations.

## Positioning

Turn screening from a filing-time checkbox into continuous, defensible risk control.

Use the customer pain first. Do not start by listing screens. Ask how the prospect handles the problem today, quantify the operational consequence, and then show the shortest product path that resolves it.

## Demo preparation

- Use an account with seeded shipments, documents, parties, and the permissions required for this category.
- Confirm the feature status shown below before a prospect call. “Roadmap” must never be presented as currently live.
- Keep one clean anchor record open before the call; avoid searching for a usable record in front of the prospect.
- After each feature, return to the customer outcome: time removed, risk reduced, revenue protected, or visibility gained.

## Restricted Party Screening

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Name-only searches create false positives, inconsistent review, and weak evidence for why a party was cleared.

### Customer benefit

Multi-source candidate generation, normalization, phonetic matching, scoring, and reviewer outcomes create a defensible decision record.

### How to demo

1. Compliance
2. Screen
3. enter a party and country
4. run screening
5. expand candidate reasoning
6. record the outcome.

**What to say:** “Multi-source candidate generation, normalization, phonetic matching, scoring, and reviewer outcomes create a defensible decision record.”

## Continuous Party Monitoring

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

A party cleared at onboarding can become restricted weeks later, while active shipments continue moving.

### Customer benefit

Qubere re-screens the party population against reference-data changes and creates focused review work only for impacted records.

### How to demo

1. Compliance
2. Monitoring
3. show last run, reference changes, impacted parties, and the resulting review queue.

**What to say:** “Qubere re-screens the party population against reference-data changes and creates focused review work only for impacted records.”

## Community Screening

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Bulk screening programs depend on brittle spreadsheets and manual collation of thousands of outcomes.

### Customer benefit

Upload CSV, XLSX, or JSON populations, validate columns, screen at scale, review exceptions, and export a single run record.

### How to demo

1. Compliance
2. Community Screening
3. upload a sample
4. map columns
5. run
6. open the run
7. export results.

**What to say:** “Upload CSV, XLSX, or JSON populations, validate columns, screen at scale, review exceptions, and export a single run record.”

## Trade-Risk Controls

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Embargo, end-use, end-user, military-use, anti-boycott, and forced-labor checks live in separate procedures.

### Customer benefit

Specialized agents apply account policies and reference data as a coordinated compliance layer on every shipment.

### How to demo

1. Open shipment
2. Compliance
3. expand each check
4. show evidence, policy version, result, and linked exception.

**What to say:** “Specialized agents apply account policies and reference data as a coordinated compliance layer on every shipment.”

## Audit, Overrides & Notifications

**Demo readiness:** Available now

### Customer pain

Overrides are often buried in email, and alerts either expose sensitive details or arrive without enough context.

### Customer benefit

Formal overrides, immutable execution history, secure review links, recipient policies, and notification settings preserve control.

### How to demo

1. Compliance
2. Audit & History
3. open an execution
4. show policy version and evidence
5. open override and notification history.

**What to say:** “Formal overrides, immutable execution history, secure review links, recipient policies, and notification settings preserve control.”

## Sales guardrails

- Do not invent ROI, accuracy, throughput, or risk-reduction percentages. Use the prospect’s baseline and approved Qubere evidence.
- If seeded data or an external connector is absent, explain the intended flow and use the product deck rather than pretending the live action completed.
